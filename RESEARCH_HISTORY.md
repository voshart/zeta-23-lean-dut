intentionally supplied no mathematical steering.

A representative prompt was literally:

```text

```

## The route that emerged

The central observation occurred inside the upstream rank-trace architecture:

1. a stronger local inequality retains actual atom norm information;
2. a leak-free relaxation replaces that information by the worst-case bound
   `1`;
3. actual six-zero Gram configurations are constrained by the sharp kernel;
4. the discarded defect can therefore be recovered locally;
5. six-phase averaging globalizes the local gain;
6. the resulting saving participates in a self-bootstrap at the endgame.

The genuinely new external finite problem eventually collapses to five
nonnegative gaps in a compact simplex.
